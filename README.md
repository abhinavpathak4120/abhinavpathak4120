## AIIMS Patna Sarthi — hospital navigation and OPD companion app

**Try it: https://abhinavpathak4120.github.io/aiims-patna-sarthi/**

A free, offline-capable web app for patients at **AIIMS Patna**. It covers OPD token and live queue, indoor department navigation, tests and lab token, reports, payment and receipt, one-tap emergency call to 108, and appointments — in Hindi, English, Bhojpuri and Maithili. 136 screens in one self-contained HTML file, no backend needed.

- **Repository:** [abhinavpathak4120/aiims-patna-sarthi](https://github.com/abhinavpathak4120/aiims-patna-sarthi)
- **One-page app guide:** [PDF](https://github.com/abhinavpathak4120/aiims-patna-sarthi/blob/main/docs/AIIMS-Patna-Sarthi-App-Guide.pdf)
- **Concept note:** [PDF](https://github.com/abhinavpathak4120/aiims-patna-sarthi/blob/main/docs/AIIMS-Patna-Sarthi-Concept-Note.pdf)
- **Pilot proposal:** [PDF](https://github.com/abhinavpathak4120/aiims-patna-sarthi/blob/main/docs/AIIMS-Patna-Sarthi-Pilot-Proposal.pdf)

Built for the **Regulatory Readiness for Global Healthcare Innovation** workshop at AIIMS Patna, organised by BIRAC (Department of Biotechnology) and the AIIMS Patna Incubation and Innovation Council, 24 October 2026.

> The app is a patient navigation and OPD workflow tool. It is not a medical device, it makes no diagnostic claim, and its AI features give general health information only.
